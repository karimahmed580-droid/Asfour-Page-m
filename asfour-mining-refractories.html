About the company

Built on precision, proven under heat

Originally established as the Egyptian Refractory Company, Asfour M&R has grown into one of the region's most reputable refractory producers. Every product line is guided by one principle passed down since the company's founding: highest in quality, greatest in service.

Our plant is built to serve a broad base of heavy industry — from steel and cement to ceramics and sugar manufacturing — with engineering teams who treat every furnace lining as a long-term partnership, not a single transaction.

  • Mission — Deliver refractory and mineral solutions engineered to outperform under sustained industrial heat.
  • Vision — To be the region's trusted refractory partner for heavy industry, worldwide.
  • Core value — Customers come first — in specification, in service, and in every shipment.
